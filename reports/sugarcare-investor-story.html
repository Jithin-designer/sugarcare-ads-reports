The Problem

Diabetes care in India is
fragmented and expensive

India has 100M+ diabetics. Most pay for care in three separate bills — medicine, doctor, lab — with no one accountable for outcomes between visits.

📊
The hidden monthly cost
A typical diabetic spends ₹750/month on medicine + ₹250 on doctor visits = ₹1,000/month. Fragmented. No structure. No follow-up.
🏥
Episodic care fails
Most doctors see a patient every 3 months. No weekly check-in. No dietitian. No accountability between visits. HbA1c stays high.
💰
Premium alternatives price out the majority
Amura charges ₹10,000/month. Strong outcomes — but inaccessible to the 90% of diabetics who can't afford it.
📍
Kerala is the highest-risk state
Kerala has among the highest diabetes prevalence per capita in South Asia, driven by the TOFI phenotype — thin outside, fat inside. The market is here.
The Solution

Structured monthly care.
Same cost. Better outcomes.

SugarCARE bundles what the patient already pays for — medicine, doctor, dietitian — into one accountable programme. The price is the same. The structure is the value.
